NP Membership Benefits

No annual fee for nonprofit organizations.

Includes directory listing, community event announcements, and access to chamber networking events.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Designed for small businesses beginning their chamber involvement.

Includes directory listing, member newsletter access, training invitations, and event discounts.

Silver Membership Benefits

Built for businesses that want stronger community visibility.

Includes Bronze benefits plus social media promotion, workshop discounts, and eligibility for home page spotlights.

Gold Membership Benefits

Best for organizations seeking premium promotion and leadership access.

Includes Silver benefits plus priority advertising, featured event placement, sponsorship opportunities, and leadership roundtables.
